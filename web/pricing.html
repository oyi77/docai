Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a setup fee?
No. Sign up, get your API key, and start verifying immediately. No setup fees, no contracts, no credit card required for the Free tier.
What happens if I exceed my monthly limit?
We notify you at 80% usage. If you exceed the limit, you can upgrade your plan at any time. Unused requests do not roll over to the next month.
Can I pay per verification (pay-as-you-go)?
Currently we offer monthly subscriptions. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom per-verification pricing. Contact us for details.
Is there a discount for annual billing?
Yes — 20% off for annual commitment. Contact our sales team to set up annual billing.
Which banks are supported?
BCA and Mandiri are production-ready. More banks are on our roadmap. Contact us if you need support for a specific bank.
Can I try before I buy?
Absolutely. The Free tier gives you 100 verifications per month — enough to test with real data and evaluate the API for your use case.
Do you offer on-premise deployment?
Yes, the Business tier includes on-premise deployment options. This is ideal for organizations with strict data residency requirements.
